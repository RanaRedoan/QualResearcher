**Download `QualResearcher-Setup-….exe` below** and install it over the old version; your transcripts, keys and settings stay as they are. New here? Read the [install guide](https://github.com/RanaRedoan/QualResearcher/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md).

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": click **More info → Run anyway**.

This is a **beta** version. Transcripts are made by AI and must be checked by a person. Found a problem? Use **Report a problem** at the bottom of the app, or email redoanhossain630@gmail.com.

### What's new in 1.0.2
- **Save as Word…** now opens the Windows "Save As" box, so you choose the folder and file name. The folder you pick is remembered.
- **Notifications (bell, top left):** a note when a transcript is ready, with a reminder to check it against the audio; a note when a job stopped and can be continued; new versions. A small pop-up appears in the corner when something new happens.
- A short reminder on every transcript that it was made with AI and must be checked.
- "Beta" label and a **Report a problem** link in the status bar.
