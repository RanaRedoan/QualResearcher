Questions

How are minutes counted?

By the length of the recording, rounded up to the next minute. If a job fails, its minutes are returned automatically.

How do I pay?

Send money by bKash, then enter the Transaction ID (TrxID) in your dashboard. Credits are added once we verify it — usually within a few hours.

How accurate is it?

Clear recordings with one person speaking at a time come out very well. Noise, echo and people talking over each other lower accuracy. Always check AI transcripts against the audio before analysis.
