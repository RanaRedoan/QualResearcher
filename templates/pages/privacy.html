Privacy & ethics

Research recordings are sensitive. This page explains what happens to them.

What we store

  • Audio — kept only until it is processed and then for the period you choose at upload (1–{{ RETENTION_MAX_DAYS }} days). After that it is deleted automatically.
  • Transcripts — kept in your account until you delete them.
  • Account details — your name, email, organization and payment records.

Who can see it

  • Only your account can open your files and transcripts. Nothing is published or shared with a public link.
  • Our administrators can see job titles and statuses to give support, but the admin panel does not display transcript text.
  • We never sell your data.

AI processing

  • Audio and text are sent to Google's Gemini API on a paid tier. Under Google's paid-tier terms, this content is not used to train their models.
  • Files are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and are not kept by us after the retention period.

Deleting your data

  • Each job has a Delete button that permanently removes its audio and transcript.
  • To close your whole account, contact us and we will delete everything linked to it.

Your responsibilities as a researcher

  • You are the data controller for your study. Before each upload you confirm that participants gave informed consent and that you work under your ethics/IRB approval and any community agreements.
  • Anonymise transcripts where your protocol requires it, and store downloads securely.
