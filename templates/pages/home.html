What you get for every recording

Built around how research teams in Bangladesh actually work with interview data.

Verbatim Bangla

Dialect, fillers and repetitions stay as spoken. English words in the speech are written in Bangla script.

English when you need it

Choose Bangla only for speed, or add a faithful English translation now or later.

Speakers labelled

Picks up where it stopped
